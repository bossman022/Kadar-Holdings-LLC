Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026  ·  Business Name: Kadar Holdings LLC  ·  Website: www.kadarholdings.com

At Kadar Holdings LLC ("Kadar Holdings," "we," "us," or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, submit a form, contact us, or use our services.

1Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address and property address
  • Property details you share with us (such as condition, occupancy, and photos)
  • Information submitted through our contact form
  • Appointment and property walkthrough details
  • Communication preferences and SMS consent records

2How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Evaluate your property and prepare an offer
  • Schedule and confirm appointments and property walkthroughs
  • Send updates about your inquiry, offer, or transaction
  • Send promotional messages only when you have expressly opted in
  • Improve our website and customer experience

3SMS Communications and Consent

Our contact form includes two optional SMS consent checkboxes. Neither box is pre-checked, and neither is required to submit the form:

  • Inquiry messages: responses, appointment updates, requested information, and transaction or status updates about your property inquiry.
  • Marketing messages: recurring property-related offers and other promotional communications.

By voluntarily checking one or both boxes, you agree to receive the selected type of text messages from Kadar Holdings LLC at the phone number you provide.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at owner@kadarholdings.com or (612) 207-8736.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase or service.

4Mobile Information and Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with trusted service providers (such as title companies, closing attorneys, and messaging providers) only as necessary to operate our business, deliver requested services, or send communications on our behalf.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

5Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

6Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.

7Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website traffic, and enhance the user experience.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
